🧠 Quick lesson

IF + present simple, present simple
Use the zero conditional for facts, rules, and things that are generally true.
🌡️ If you heat ice, it melts.
A scientific fact.
🌱 If plants don't get water, they die.
A general truth.
😴 If I go to bed late, I feel tired.
A usual result.

💡 Remember: You can switch the order. “Plants die if they don't get water.” No comma is needed when the if-clause comes second.
